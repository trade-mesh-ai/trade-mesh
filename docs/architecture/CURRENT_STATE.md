**Bug 1 — RESOLVED: current-value quantity scaling and netting mismatch.** `GET /trading/positions/open` returned `liveUnrealizedPnl` at exactly half the correct `unrealizedPnl` for this 2-lot position. ## `feature/position_sizing_vix_lot_multiplier` — full summary (2026-07-09)

**Starting point:** all four strategies traded a hardcoded `max-lots: 1` regardless of account capital, market volatility, or trade quality — explicitly flagged in README's own Phase 3 roadmap as needing capital-based sizing eventually. This branch built that out in three layers, for Iron Condor and (partially) the two spreads.

**Note (2026-07-15): confirmed during design review that this three-layer sizing system had likely never been observably active in production** — `LotSizeService.getPositionSize()`'s final step caps the computed result at `cfg.getMaxLots()`, which was hardcoded to `1` on every strategy until Kuldeep raised it to real values (`max-lots: 30`, `account-capital: 2000000`) on 2026-07-15, separately from the design review implementation work. The sizing math below was genuinely built and unit-tested correctly; it was simply clamped to always output 1 lot in practice until this config change.

### Layer 1 — Capital-based sizing (all three defined-risk strategies: Iron Condor, Bull Put Spread, Bear Call Spread)

Found and fixed a real circular-dependency bug in passing: the existing `maxLoss = (wingWidth - netCredit) × lotSize × lots` formula couldn't be evaluated before `lots` was known, but `lots` was being derived *from* `maxLoss` in the new design. Fixed by separating `maxLossPerLot` (risk of one lot, computable immediately after `netCredit`) from the final `maxLoss` (computed *after* `lots` is determined). Added `LotSizeService.getPositionSize(BigDecimal maxLossPerLot)`: `lots = floor((accountCapital × riskPercent) / maxLossPerLot)`, floored at 1, capped at `cfg.getMaxLots()` (which now serves as a safety ceiling, not the source of truth). New top-level config: `trading.account-capital`, `trading.risk-percent-per-trade`. `ExpiryDayStrategy` explicitly excluded — it's a naked strangle with no `wingWidth`-bounded max loss, needs its own design discussion (see Tracked Gaps).

### Layer 2 — VIX-based sizing (Iron Condor, Bull Put Spread, Bear Call Spread)

New `getPositionSize(maxLossPerLot, currentVix)` overload: VIX>18 scales to 0.5x, VIX≤18 stays full size, floored at 1 lot even after scaling. Required threading `currentVix` as a new parameter into `IronCondorStrategy.selectStrikesAndBuildSignal()` — it was declared in `evaluate()`'s outer lambda but that method is a separate private method with its own scope, discovered only after a real compile error (assumed-in-scope claims should always be verified, not trusted).

**The accompanying integration test (`IronCondorStrategyVixScalingTest`) required five distinct rounds of real debugging** before passing — a good case study in not accepting "should work" reasoning:
1. A wall-clock-dependent hardcoded `latestEntryTime: "15:00"` in the test's mock config caused every test to fail if run after 3 PM (same bug class as two earlier date-hardcoding incidents this session).
2. The mock option chain's uniform flat LTPs produced exactly zero net credit, correctly rejected by the strategy's min-credit gate — invisible until Standard Output logs were actually read.
3. The initial strike range (±500 from spot) was too narrow for the delta-based strike selector to find a valid far-OTM short strike at all.
4. A stale cached HTML test report was mistakenly read as current output, showing an unrelated "3 open positions" message from a different test run — resolved by re-running fresh via `--info` console output instead of trusting the report file.
5. ### Layer 3 — Confidence-based sizing (Iron Condor only)

New `getPositionSize(maxLossPerLot, currentVix, confidenceScore, minScore)` 4-arg overload, composing VIX-scaling and confidence-scaling multiplicatively. Formula: `multiplier = clamp(0.5 + (score - minScore) / (2 × (100 - minScore)), 0.5, 1.0)`.

**Discovered a real, separate architectural gap in the process:** `BullPutSpreadStrategy`/`BearCallSpreadStrategy` have no confidence-scoring mechanism at all — deferred as its own session (later resolved 2026-07-14, see below).

### Principle established: sizing-vs-scoring double-count

VIX was being used **three times simultaneously**: hard entry gate, confidence-score input, and sizing multiplier. **Established principle: a factor may have a hard gate plus at most ONE graded mechanism.** VIX removed from Iron Condor's confidence score (85-point ceiling), `min-score` recalibrated 60→51.
